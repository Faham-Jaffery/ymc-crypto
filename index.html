Project status & disclaimer

Yuaan Musk Coin (YMC) is currently a conceptual blueprint and pre-build infrastructure project. No live Layer-1 network, testnet, deployed token contract, consensus implementation, or benchmark results are represented by this website. The DPoS and IBFT 2.0 model, EVM compatibility, ~1.2-second block-time target, and up-to-3,500 TPS figure are proposed design assumptions for evaluation only, not verified performance claims. Tokenomics, allocations, vesting schedules, emissions, and governance mechanisms are preliminary and remain subject to technical feasibility review, independent security audits where applicable, legal review, and approval through the project's eventual governance process. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell securities or tokens, investment advice, or a promise of returns. Specifications and milestones may change before any testnet or mainnet deployment.
